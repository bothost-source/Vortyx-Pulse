FAQ

Common questions

What happens when my free plan expires? +

Your API keys stop accepting requests 30 days after signup. Your account and key history stay intact — upgrading to any paid plan reactivates access immediately.

Can I have more than 5 keys on the free plan? +

No — the free plan is capped at 5 API keys. Any paid plan (3-month, annual, or permanent) removes that limit entirely.

How long do crypto payments take to confirm? +

Up to 12 hours, usually faster. Our team manually verifies each transaction on-chain before activating your plan.

Do all plans use the same model? +

Yes. Every plan gives you the same vortyx-1 model across text, audio, and video — plans only affect duration, key limits, and support priority.

Can I revoke or rotate an API key? +

Yes, anytime from your dashboard. Revoked keys stop working immediately and can't be reactivated — create a new one instead.
