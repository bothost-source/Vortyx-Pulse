Platform

Three modalities. One model, one key, one bill.

Most providers make you stitch together separate APIs for text, speech, and vision. Vortyx Pulse handles all three natively, so your product ships faster and your infra stays simple.

01 / TEXT

Chat & reasoning

Long-context conversation, function calling, and structured output from a single completions endpoint.

02 / AUDIO

Voice in, voice out

Stream speech directly into the model and get natural spoken responses back — no separate transcription step.

03 / VIDEO

Frame-level vision

Send video and ask what happened, when, and why — the model reasons across frames, not just stills.
